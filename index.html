about the webmaster

hi! i'm a student interested in computer science, poetry, photography, and the odd little details people tend to walk past. i made this website to learn how the web works while building a home for my creative projects.

this site is intentionally old-fashioned. the early web felt like walking into someone's bedroom and finding a shelf full of things they cared about. i'd like this place to feel a little like that.

webmaster's note

this site was created for me to build a digital, unfiltered, and informal portfolio of some of my favorite things. this site is also designed to mimic an old 90s or y2k style site since websites from that era are visually engaging and fun to interact with. stuff may be added and/or removed.

Thanks for visiting!!
