about the webmaster

hi! my name is landon, and i'm a student interested in computer science, poetry, photography, and storytelling. i designed this website as a place to host some of my favorites from my work and also share a bit about myself. i also figured this would be a fun project, since i've been wanting to make my own website for years.

this site is intentionally old-fashioned. i have explored some of what remains from the old web, and i really like the seemingly unconventional design choices. it feels like each site was someone's own, so i chose to design this site with that style in mind because it resonates with me.

webmaster's note

this site was created for me to build a digital, unfiltered, and informal portfolio of some of my favorite things. depending on when you are viewing this, there may be a secret somewhere on the site. stuff may be added and/or removed. generative ai was used to create the code for this site; however, every design choice was made by me personally, and no ai was used in any of the creative works presented.

thanks for visiting!!
